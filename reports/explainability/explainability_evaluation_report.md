# AGRI-X Explainability, Attribution & Robustness Evaluation Report

## 1. Executive Summary
This report documents the Explainable AI (XAI) methodology implemented in **Phase 3** of the AGRI-X agricultural diagnostic system. By coupling **Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping)** with confidence-calibrated probability distributions, the system provides transparent, interpretable visual evidence for disease predictions generated by the deep neural network.

## 3. Misclassification Attribution Analysis
Out of 2,732 held-out test images evaluated in Phase 2, the ResNet18 model committed **43 errors** (1.57% total error rate):
- **High-Confidence Errors ($\ge 80\%$ Softmax Confidence)**: 16 instances.
- **Low-Confidence / Ambiguous Errors ($< 80\%$ Softmax Confidence)**: 27 instances.

### Key Attribution Observations:
1. **Target Spot vs. Early Blight**:
   - Both diseases present concentric circular ring lesions ("target-like" halos). The Grad-CAM attribution shows the model concentrates on the necrotic center of the lesion; however, without high-resolution microscopic spore inspection, fungal spot morphology overlaps significantly in early vegetative stages.
2. **Spider Mite Stippling vs. Yellow Leaf Curl**:
   - Spider mite feeding causes tiny yellow chlorotic specks. In diffuse lighting, heatmaps for low-confidence spider mite errors exhibit broad, unlocalized activations spanning the upper lamina rather than sharp lesion foci.
3. **Counterfactual Gradient Analysis**:
   - By querying Grad-CAM for the ground-truth class on misclassified images, we observe that the gradients for the true pathology fail to form coherent spatial clusters, confirming the model lacked salient spatial cues for the true class in those specific frames.

## 4. Controlled Perturbation & Robustness Experiments
To assess attribution and prediction stability, non-destructive transformations were applied across representative samples:

| Perturbation Type | Description | Prediction Retention Rate | Mean Confidence Drift |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Baseline** | Unmodified test images | **100.0%** | -0.0029 |
| **Brightness (+25%)** | Simulated strong daylight | **90.0%** | +0.0169 |
| **Brightness (-25%)** | Overcast / shadow lighting | **100.0%** | +0.0067 |
| **Contrast (+25%)** | Harsh illumination edges | **80.0%** | -0.0251 |
| **Contrast (-25%)** | Low dynamic range camera | **100.0%** | +0.0368 |
| **Rotation ($10^\circ$)** | Handheld mobile tilt | **90.0%** | -0.0557 |
| **Mild Blur (Gaussian $\sigma=1.2$)** | Mobile camera motion blur | **80.0%** | -0.0048 |
| **Downscale ($160\times160$)** | Low-resolution camera sensor | **80.0%** | +0.0267 |

### Robustness Insights:
- The model exhibits highest resilience to luminance shifts (brightness reduction maintained 100% classification retention).
- Contrast elevation and image downscaling cause the steepest drop in prediction retention (80%), emphasizing the need for high-resolution input preprocessing in the Phase 4/5 deployment pipelines.

## 5. Methodological Limitations of Grad-CAM
1. **Attribution, Not Proof of Biological Causation**:
   - A Grad-CAM activation indicates where the convolutional filters responded most intensely to optimize class logit scores. It does *not* prove that the highlighted region is the biological etiology of the disease.
2. **Coarse Spatial Resolution**:
   - ResNet18 downsamples input images ($224 \times 224$) through 5 pooling/stride stages down to $7 \times 7$ feature maps in `layer4`. Bilinear upsampling to $224 \times 224$ produces smooth, rounded heatmaps that cannot delineate microscopic bacterial oozes or fine fungal hyphae.
3. **Sensitivity to Multiple Lesions**:
   - When multiple lesions are present across the leaf, Grad-CAM frequently peaks on a single dominant focal area rather than uniformly highlighting all infected patches.
4. **Softmax Confidence Calibration**:
   - A 99% Softmax score is a relative ranking among the 10 closed-set training classes, not an absolute statistical guarantee of real-world infection.
